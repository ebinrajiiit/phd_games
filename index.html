IIIT Kottayam, Research Methodology

Augmented, Not Automated

Three short games for the session on doing a PhD in the age of AI agents.

  • Reference Hunt3 minutes Ten references, some real and some invented. Spot the fakes in 15 seconds each.
  • Human or Machine?4 minutes Paragraphs from paper introductions. Decide whether a researcher or an LLM wrote each one.
  • Prompt Duel3 minutes, in pairs Rebuild a weak prompt into a research-grade one, then put it to the room's vote.

Your answers stay on your phone. Only your nickname and score go to the room screen.
